# HackClub-APPA
This is a Robot appa, complete with the 6 legs, giant tail, fur, and his love of food. 

Appa is almost 3d printed and runs off of Python. It uses an LED screen to power his personality, and he may even come with airbending (using a fan).

I came up with the idea of robot Appa because of the new ATLA movie. I thought it would be a fun costume accessory if the movie comes to the theaters. He will also have a mouth to pick up and eat fruit, because a fruit-delivering robot would also be pretty cool.
